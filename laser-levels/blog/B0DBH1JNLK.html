Key Features

The sturdy and versatile tripod for laser level included in the set can extend from 17” to 50 in height, helping you adjust the position and height of the laser line level. The magnetic bracket included in the set can rotate 360 degrees and firmly adhere to the metal surface.
  • [Self-Leveling & Manual Mode] For Self-Leveling mode: unlock the pendulum, the Huepar 360 self leveling laser level will project vertical or horizontal laser lines when the tilt angle is less than 3°, and the laser will continue to flash when it exceeds 3°. The accuracy is ±1/9" at 33 feet. Lock the pendulum and switch to manual mode, and the laser level will project a straight line to meet your alignment needs at different angles.
  • [Pulse Mode] Pulse mode extends the working distance of the laser level from 98 feet to 196 feet. In pulse mode, the laser lines flicker at a very high frequency, making them detectable by laser receivers. It is very suitable for outdoor use with low laser visibility or working distances exceeding 98 feet. (Note: Pulse mode requires the use of Huepar LR-6RG/LR-5RG receiver, which needs to be purchased separately.)
  • [Rechargeable Lithium-Ion Battery] The built-in 3.7V 4000mAh lithium-ion battery can work continuously for 8 hours after fully charged. The four battery indicator lights display the current battery status, and the charging protection prevents overcharging. This laser tool can also be charged by laptop, power bank, car charger, etc. because it has a type-C charging port.
