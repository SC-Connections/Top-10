Product Overview

The TQ1 Smart Thermostat connects to the internet through a Matter-enabled smart-home speaker and is the easiest to use connected thermostat. It connects directly with your smart-speaker allowing local and remote control via your existing smart-home app. Use of the Matter IoT protocol means you are never locked-in to a single App, simply change your smart-speaker to change your smart-home. All system configuration done at device, no app required powered by the Matter protocol. Everything works with the smart-home app already on your phone, no new accounts or apps to download. Use the Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa or SmartThings App to set-up the TQ1 in seconds. A smart speaker or Matter controller is required for connectivity outside the home..
